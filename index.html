Hozir

The one thing worth doing right now.

Hozir means “now.” It looks at your tasks, your calendar and your priorities for the week, then picks your next task and tells you why in a single line.

  • Say what’s on your mind and it becomes tasks, with dates and times filled in.
  • Set up to three priorities for the week. Linked tasks get picked sooner.
  • Focus timer with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Calendar and Reminders access is read-only. AI is optional and off by default.
Download on the App Store Free · iPhone · iOS 15.1+

DoorsAway

Buy a rental that pays for itself, in a market you don’t live in.

For owners buying far from home. DoorsAway shows where rent covers the mortgage, explains why each neighborhood works, and points you to property managers and agents who work with out-of-state owners.

  • Estimated monthly cash flow by ZIP and property type, with yield and price trend.
  • Move the interest rate and vacancy and watch the ranking change.
  • Every number sourced: rent comps, taxes, insurance and risk.
  • Market reports for Memphis and Cleveland.

How we work

A few rules we follow at the workbench.

  1. 01

    Scratch our own itch

    We build what we need ourselves and use it every day. If it doesn’t help us, it won’t ship.

  2. 02

    Small on purpose

    Fewer products, fewer features, fewer meetings. Staying small keeps us close to the work and to the people who use it.

  3. 03

    Calm over urgent

    No manufactured deadlines and no dark patterns. Software should respect your attention, and so should the people who make it.

  4. 04

    Made by hand, kept for years

    An usta stands behind their work. We look after what we ship long after launch day.

  5. 05

    Plain and honest

    Clear pricing, clear words, clear data practices. If something changes, we’ll tell you directly.

  6. 06

    One name per thing

    Every product gets a name that means something. Many of them come from Uzbek, the language we grew up with.

About

Why “Ustahona”?

In Uzbek, an usta is a master craftsman, and an ustaxona is their workshop: the room where the tools hang on the wall and the work gets done properly.

That’s what we want this company to be. Ustahona was started by Jahon Amirkulov, a software engineer who grew up in Uzbekistan and now builds from the San Francisco Bay Area. Every product here starts as a tool he wanted for himself.
